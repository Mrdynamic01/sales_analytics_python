# Sales & Business Analytics — Python

## Overview
Analysis of 9,800 retail orders worth $2.26M in revenue to uncover 
business insights and performance trends.

## Tools Used
- Python, Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings
- Business grew 30% in 2017, decelerating to 20% in 2018
- Technology is the top performing category
- Phones drive the highest sub-category revenue
- November & December are peak sales months
- West region and Corporate segment lead in revenue

## Files
- `sales_analysis.ipynb` — Full analysis notebook
- `train.csv` — Dataset
